# Context-dependent weighting: calibration report

Generated by `eval/calibrate_weights.py` on 2026-11-20. Re-run it after changing any analyzer or adding sources.

**Decision:** kept **static** weights: no dynamic candidate was non-inferior on held-out sources without raising the false-positive rate.

## Batch

- 18 labeled sources: 3 generated, 15 genuine.
- 648 synthetic variants (36 per source): rescale 0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0 x save PNG / JPEG 95, 90, 80, 70, 60, plus 320px centre crops (PNG, JPEG 85), grayscale (PNG, JPEG 85), JPEG 90 then resize 0.75, double JPEG 85 -> 75.
- Generated sources: `fireplace-generated`, `ai-generated`, `ai-retouched`.
- Genuine sources: `real-photo`, `astronaut`, `brick`, `camera`, `chelsea`, `china`, `coins`, `flower`, `grace_hopper`, `grass`, `gravel`, `moon`, `motorcycle_left`, `motorcycle_right`, `rocket`.

## Caveats

- Only 3 generated sources, each caught by a different signal. That is why only degradation behaviour is fitted: a combiner that learns which signal means AI inverts on held-out generators. Adding generated images with `--ai-dir` (and large, colour genuine photos with `--real-dir`, which would make size and color learnable) is the most valuable improvement; everything here is re-derived.
- Degradations are synthetic stand-ins for real pipelines (social-media recompression, screenshots, CDN resizing). They cover the mechanisms, not every encoder.
- The batch's transform mix is itself an assumption about real traffic. For example, 40% of variants are upscaled, for generated and genuine sources alike, which is why the frequency knee (an upscaling detector) separates nothing across the batch as a whole.
- Weighting can only re-balance evidence the signals measured. Most misses in the tables above are variants where every tell was destroyed (downscaling, JPEG on a noise-only source); no weighting recovers those.
